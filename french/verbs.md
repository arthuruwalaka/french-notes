# Essential verbs

[← Study index](../README.md)

French present tense can express both a habit and an action happening now. For example, **je fais** can mean “I do” or “I am doing.”

<a id="etre"></a>
## Être — to be

**être** sounds like **eh-truh**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **suis** | swee |
| tu | **es** | eh |
| il / elle | **est** | eh |
| nous | **sommes** | sum |
| vous | **êtes** | et |
| ils / elles | **sont** | sohn (nasal) |

Past participle: **été**. *J’ai été* — I was / I have been.

Use **être** for identity, description, and location:

- Je suis canadien.
- Elle est française.
- Nous sommes à l’école.

Use the infinitive after another verb: *Je veux **être** médecin.* — I want to be a doctor.

Imperfect — **was / used to be**:

| Subject | Form | Sounds like | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **j’étais** | zhay-teh | I was |
| tu | **tu étais** | too ay-teh | you were |
| il / elle / on | **était** | ay-teh | he/she/one was |
| nous | **nous étions** | noo-zay-tee-ohn | we were |
| vous | **vous étiez** | voo-zay-tee-ay | you were |
| ils / elles | **ils étaient** | eel-zay-teh | they were |

- **J’étais fatigué.** — I was tired.
- **Nous étions à l’école.** — We were at school.

Key forms:

- **est** — is
- **était** — was/used to be (imperfect)
- **été** — been (past participle): *j’ai été* — I was / I have been

<a id="avoir"></a>
## Avoir — to have

**avoir** sounds like **ah-vwahr**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **ai → j’ai** | jay |
| tu | **as** | ah |
| il / elle | **a** | ah |
| nous | **avons** | ah-vohn |
| vous | **avez** | ah-vay |
| ils / elles | **ont** | ohn (nasal) |

Past participle: **eu** (u). *J’ai eu* — I had / I have had.

Use **avoir** for possession and age:

- J’ai un chat.
- Elle a vingt ans. — She is 20 years old.

Write **j’ai**, never *je ai*.

<a id="aller"></a>
## Aller — to go

**aller** sounds like **ah-lay**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **vais** | veh |
| tu | **vas** | vah |
| il / elle | **va** | vah |
| nous | **allons** | ah-lohn |
| vous | **allez** | ah-lay |
| ils / elles | **vont** | vohn (nasal) |

Past participle: **allé / allée / allés / allées**. *Je suis allé; elle est allée.* — I went; she went.

- Je vais à l’école.
- Elle va à la maison.
- Nous allons à Paris.

**aller + infinitive** forms the near future: *Il va pleuvoir* = “It is going to rain.”

The passé composé uses **être**, not **avoir**:

- **Je suis allé.** — I went. (not *J’ai allé*)
- **Elle est allée.** — She went.

**y** replaces **à + a place** already known, and sits before the auxiliary:

- Je suis allé à Paris deux fois. → **J’y suis allé deux fois.** — I went there twice.
- Word order: **Je + y + suis + allé**
- **suis** here is the auxiliary (“have gone”), not present “I am going.”

**y** vs **là / là-bas**:

- **J’y vais.** / **J’y retourne.** — I’m going / going back there. (**y** before the verb)
- **Je retourne là-bas.** — I’m going back over there.
- **Il est là.** — He is there / here (present).
- Wrong: *Je suis y*; *Je retourne y*

<a id="faire"></a>
## Faire — to do / to make

**faire** sounds like **fehr**. It does not mean “to take”; **prendre** means “to take.”

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **fais** | feh |
| tu | **fais** | feh |
| il / elle | **fait** | feh |
| nous | **faisons** | fuh-zohn |
| vous | **faites** | fet |
| ils / elles | **font** | fohn (nasal) |

Past participle: **fait**. *J’ai fait* — I did / I have done.

- Je fais la vaisselle. — I do / I am doing the dishes.
- Il fait un gâteau. — He makes / is making a cake.
- Il fait beau. — The weather is nice.
- Ça fait peur. — That is scary / That causes fear.
- Ça fait rire. — That makes people laugh.

Recognize the whole pattern:

- **faire + noun** — do/make: *faire un gâteau*
- **faire + infinitive** — cause: *faire rire; faire enrager*
- **il fait + weather** — weather condition: *il fait froid*

**faisant** (fuh-zahn) means “doing/making”:

- Il écoute de la musique **en faisant** la vaisselle.
- Not: *Je faisant*

## Mettre — to put / to put on

**mettre** sounds like **meh-truh**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **mets** | meh |
| tu | **mets** | meh |
| il / elle | **met** | meh |
| nous | **mettons** | meh-tohn |
| vous | **mettez** | meh-tay |
| ils / elles | **mettent** | met |

Past participle: **mis** (mee). *J’ai mis* — I put / I have put.

- **Je mets le livre ici.** — I am putting the book here.
- **Elle met son manteau.** — She is putting on her coat.
- **Mets ça ici.** — Put that here.

- **J’ai mis le livre ici.** — I put the book here.
- **Où as-tu mis mon livre ?** — Where did you put my book?

**mets** is the present. **mis** is the past participle.

**se mettre à** = to start doing: **Il se met à pleuvoir.** — It starts raining.

## Venir — to come

**venir** sounds like **vuh-neer**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **viens** | vyehn (nasal) |
| tu | **viens** | vyehn (nasal) |
| il / elle | **vient** | vyehn (nasal) |
| nous | **venons** | vuh-nohn |
| vous | **venez** | vuh-nay |
| ils / elles | **viennent** | vyen |

Past participle: **venu / venue**. *Il est venu; elle est venue.* — He came; she came.

Commands:

- **Viens !** — one person, informal
- **Venez !** — formal or multiple people
- Venez ici, s’il vous plaît.

**venir de + infinitive** means “to have just done something”:

- Je viens de manger. — I just ate.

### Same pattern as venir

These reuse **viens / vient / venons / venu**. Learn the meaning; do not memorize a new table.

| Verb | Meaning | Past participle | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **devenir** | to become | **devenu** (être) | **Je suis devenu bibliothécaire.** — I became a librarian. |
| **revenir** | to come back | **revenu** (être) | **Il est revenu.** — He came back. |
| **prévenir** | to warn / let know | **prévenu** | **J’ai prévenu Nico.** — I let Nico know. |
| **se souvenir de** | to remember | **souvenu** (être) | **Je me suis souvenu de toi.** — I remembered you. |
| **parvenir à** | to manage to / to reach | **parvenu** (être) | **Elle est parvenue à finir.** — She managed to finish. |

**venir**, **devenir**, **revenir**, and **parvenir** use **être**. **prévenir** uses **avoir**.

<a id="savoir"></a>
## Savoir — to know

**savoir** sounds like **sah-vwahr**. Use it for facts, information, and knowing how to do something.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **sais** | seh |
| tu | **sais** | seh |
| il / elle | **sait** | seh |
| nous | **savons** | sah-vohn |
| vous | **savez** | sah-vay |
| ils / elles | **savent** | sahv |

Past participle: **su**. *J’ai su* — I knew / I found out.

- Je sais. — I know.
- Je sais cuisiner. — I know how to cook.
- Tu sais où il habite ? — Do you know where he lives?

**Savoir vs connaître**

- **savoir** = know a fact or know how: *Je sais cuisiner.*
- **connaître** = know or be familiar with a person/place: *Je connais Paris.*

<a id="vouloir"></a>
## Vouloir — to want

**vouloir** sounds like **voo-lwahr**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **veux** | vuh |
| tu | **veux** | vuh |
| il / elle | **veut** | vuh |
| nous | **voulons** | voo-lohn |
| vous | **voulez** | voo-lay |
| ils / elles | **veulent** | vuhl |

Past participle: **voulu**. *J’ai voulu* — I wanted / I have wanted.

Use **vouloir + noun** or **vouloir + infinitive**:

- Je veux du riz. — I want some rice.
- Je veux manger. — I want to eat.
- Vous voulez venir ? — Do you want to come?

For a polite request, **je voudrais** means “I would like”:

- Je voudrais un café, s’il vous plaît. — I would like a coffee, please.

Compare:

- **je veux** — I want
- **je voudrais** — I would like (conditional/polite)
- **je voudrai** — I will want (future)
- **j’ai voulu** — I wanted / I have wanted

<a id="devoir"></a>
## Devoir — to have to / must

**devoir** sounds like **duh-vwahr**. Use **devoir + infinitive**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **dois** | dwah |
| tu | **dois** | dwah |
| il / elle | **doit** | dwah |
| nous | **devons** | duh-vohn |
| vous | **devez** | duh-vay |
| ils / elles | **doivent** | dwahv |

Past participle: **dû**. *J’ai dû partir* — I had to leave.

- **Je dois partir.** — I have to leave.
- **Tu dois prévenir Nico.** — You have to let Nico know.
- **Nous devons changer 50 dollars.** — We have to exchange 50 dollars.

**devoir** can also mean **to owe**:

- **Je te dois 10 euros.** — I owe you 10 euros.

Key forms:

- **je dois** — I have to / I must
- **je devrais** — I should
- **j’ai dû** — I had to
- **j’aurais dû** — I should have

- **Je devrais prendre la purée.** — I should get the mash.
- **J’aurais dû le prendre.** — I should have taken it.

**dû** is the past participle. Do not confuse it with **du** = **de + le**:

- **J’ai dû partir.** — I had to leave.
- **J’ai du pain.** — I have some bread.

<a id="falloir"></a>
## Falloir — to be necessary

**falloir** sounds like **fah-lwahr**. It is used only with **il**. There is no **je faut**.

| Form | Sounds like | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **il faut** | eel fo | it is necessary / you have to |
| **il me faut** | eel muh fo | I need |
| **il faut que** | eel fo kuh | it is necessary that |
| **il faudra** | eel fo-drah | it will be necessary |
| **il faudrait** | eel fo-dreh | it would be necessary / should |
| **il a fallu** | eel ah fah-lü | it was necessary |

Past participle: **fallu**. *Il a fallu* — it was necessary.

- **Il faut partir.** — We have to leave.
- **Il me faut un café.** — I need a coffee.
- **Il me faut juste cinq minutes.** — I just need five minutes.
- **Il faut que je parte.** — I have to leave.  
  **il faut que** takes the subjunctive.

Compare:

- **J’ai besoin d’un café.** — I need a coffee.
- **Il me faut un café.** — I need a coffee.
- Wrong: *je faut*

## Partir — to leave

**partir** sounds like **par-teer**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **pars** | par |
| tu | **pars** | par |
| il / elle | **part** | par |
| nous | **partons** | par-tohn |
| vous | **partez** | par-tay |
| ils / elles | **partent** | part |

Past participle: **parti / partie**. *Il est parti; elle est partie.* — He left; she left.

- Il part maintenant. — He is leaving now.
- Nous partons demain. — We are leaving tomorrow.

**à partir de** is not this verb. It means “starting from”:

- **à partir de lundi** — starting Monday
- **Il est temps de partir.** — It’s time to leave.

## Regular -er verbs — parler, aider, aimer, and donner

Most French verbs follow the **-er** pattern. Remove **-er**, then add:

| Subject | Ending | Parler — to speak | Aider — to help |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| je | **-e** | je parle | j’aide |
| tu | **-es** | tu parles | tu aides |
| il / elle | **-e** | il parle | elle aide |
| nous | **-ons** | nous parlons | nous aidons |
| vous | **-ez** | vous parlez | vous aidez |
| ils / elles | **-ent** | ils parlent | elles aident |

Past participle: drop **-er**, add **-é**. **parlé, aidé, aimé, donné, mangé**. *J’ai parlé* — I spoke.

The endings **-e, -es,** and **-ent** are normally silent.

- Je parle français. — I speak French.
- Il aide sa mère. — He helps his mother.
- J’aime le français. — I like French.
- Donne-moi ça. — Give me that.
- J’habite en France. — I live in France.
- Je regarde la télé. — I watch television.
- J’écoute la radio. — I listen to the radio.

Before a vowel or silent **h**, **je** shortens to **j’**: *j’aime; j’aide; j’habite; j’écoute*.

### Manger spelling

**manger** follows the regular **-er** endings, but keeps an extra **e** in **nous mangeons** so the **g** retains its “zh” sound:

- je mange
- tu manges
- il/elle mange
- **nous mangeons**
- vous mangez
- ils/elles mangent

### Appeler spelling

**appeler** means “to call.” It doubles the **l** in most present forms:

| Subject | Form |
| --- | --- |
| je | **j’appelle** |
| tu | **tu appelles** |
| il / elle | **il appelle** |
| nous | **nous appelons** |
| vous | **vous appelez** |
| ils / elles | **ils appellent** |

Past participle: **appelé**. *J’ai appelé* — I called.

- Je l’appelle tout de suite. — I’ll call him/her right away.
- Je m’appelle Arthur. — My name is Arthur. (literally: I call myself Arthur)

## Voir — to see / to understand

**voir** sounds like **vwahr**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **vois** | vwah |
| tu | **vois** | vwah |
| il / elle | **voit** | vwah |

- Je vois. — I see / I understand.

### Voir, regarder, and écouter

- **voir** = to see: *Je vois un oiseau.*
- **regarder** = to look at/watch: *Je regarde la télé.*
- **écouter** = to listen to: *J’écoute la radio.*

Unlike English, **regarder** and **écouter** take their object directly—do not add a word for “at” or “to.”

<a id="tenir"></a>
## Tenir — to hold / to keep

**tenir** sounds like **tuh-neer**.

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je | **tiens** | tyehn |
| tu | **tiens** | tyehn |
| il / elle | **tient** | tyehn |
| nous | **tenons** | tuh-nohn |
| vous | **tenez** | tuh-nay |
| ils / elles | **tiennent** | tyehn |

Past participle: **tenu**. *J’ai tenu* — I held / I have held.

- **Je tiens le sac.** — I’m holding the bag.
- **Tiens !** — Here! / Take this!
- **Tenez.** — Here. Formal or plural.

**tenir à** means to care about, or to insist on:

- **Je tiens à toi.** — You matter to me.

**tiens** is a form of **tenir**. **le tien** means “yours” and is a different word.

Same stem as **tenir** (**tiens / tient / tenu**), meanings only: **obtenir** to get; **retenir** to keep / remember; **appartenir à** to belong to; **soutenir** to support.

## Sentir — to feel / to smell

| Subject | Form | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |

Past participle: **manqué**. *J’ai manqué le train* — I missed the train.

Two common patterns:

- miss an event or object: **Je manque le train.** — I’m missing the train.
- miss a person or place: the thing missed is the subject  
  **L’Espagne me manque.** — I miss Spain.  
  Literally: Spain is missing to me.

- **Tu me manques.** — I miss you.
- **Il me manque un stylo.** — I’m missing a pen. / I need a pen.
