# Numbers

[← Study index](../README.md)

Do not learn 1–100 as a long list. Learn **three layers**, then reuse them.

## How to learn them

1. Memorize **0–20**. These are the building blocks.
2. Reuse 1–9 after the tens **20–60**.
3. Treat **70–99** as math, not new words:
   - **70–79** = 60 + 10–19
   - **80–99** = 4 × 20 + 0–19

Daily drill: say **0–20**, then **21, 31, 41, 51, 61**, then **70, 71, 80, 81, 90, 91**.

## Layer 1: 0–20

Memorize these. Pronunciations are English approximations.

| Number | French | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | **zéro** | zay-roh |
| 1 | **un** | uhn |
| 2 | **deux** | duh |
| 3 | **trois** | trwah |
| 4 | **quatre** | katr |
| 5 | **cinq** | sank |
| 6 | **six** | seess |
| 7 | **sept** | set |
| 8 | **huit** | weet |
| 9 | **neuf** | nuhf |
| 10 | **dix** | deess |
| 11 | **onze** | onz |
| 12 | **douze** | dooz |
| 13 | **treize** | trez |
| 14 | **quatorze** | kah-torz |
| 15 | **quinze** | kanz |
| 16 | **seize** | sez |
| 17 | **dix-sept** | deess-set |
| 18 | **dix-huit** | deez-weet |
| 19 | **dix-neuf** | deez-nuhf |
| 20 | **vingt** | van |

**17–19** are already built: **dix + sept/huit/neuf**.

## Layer 2: 20–69

Learn the tens, then add 1–9:

| Ten | French | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | **vingt** | van |
| 30 | **trente** | trahnt |
| 40 | **quarante** | kah-rahnt |
| 50 | **cinquante** | sank-ahnt |
| 60 | **soixante** | swah-sahnt |

Pattern:

- **22** **vingt-deux** — twenty-two
- **45** **quarante-cinq** — forty-five
- **58** **cinquante-huit** — fifty-eight

**21, 31, 41, 51, 61** use **et**:

- **21** **vingt et un** — twenty-one
- **31** **trente et un** — thirty-one

Other numbers use a hyphen: **vingt-deux**, **trente-trois**.

## Layer 3: 70–99

No new “seventy / eighty / ninety” words in standard French. Reuse 10–19 and 4 × 20.

| Number | Think | French |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 70 | 60 + 10 | **soixante-dix** |
| 71 | 60 + 11 | **soixante et onze** |
| 72 | 60 + 12 | **soixante-douze** |
| 80 | 4 × 20 | **quatre-vingts** |
| 81 | 4 × 20 + 1 | **quatre-vingt-un** |
| 90 | 4 × 20 + 10 | **quatre-vingt-dix** |
| 91 | 4 × 20 + 11 | **quatre-vingt-onze** |
| 99 | 4 × 20 + 19 | **quatre-vingt-dix-neuf** |

- **71** keeps **et**: **soixante et onze**
- **81** has no **et**: **quatre-vingt-un**
- **80** has a final **s**: **quatre-vingts**
- After 80, drop the **s**: **quatre-vingt-un**, **quatre-vingt-dix**

## 100 and up

| Number | French | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | **cent** | no **un** before it: not *un cent* |
| 101 | **cent un** | no hyphen needed here |
| 200 | **deux cents** | **s** on **cents** |
| 201 | **deux cent un** | no **s** when another number follows |
| 1 000 | **mille** | never **un mille**; **mille** does not take **s** |
| 2 000 | **deux mille** | |
| 1 000 000 | **un million** | **million** is a noun, so **un** is required |

- **365** **trois cent soixante-cinq** — three hundred sixty-five
- **2026** **deux mille vingt-six** — two thousand twenty-six

## Useful number patterns

Age uses **avoir**, not **être**:

- **J’ai vingt ans.** — I am 20 years old.
- **Elle a quatre-vingts ans.** — She is 80 years old.

Clock time:

- **Il est une heure.** — It is 1:00.
- **Il est deux heures.** — It is 2:00.
- **à 21 heures** — at 9 p.m.

Dates use masculine **le**, because **jour** is understood:

- **le 2 septembre** — September 2
- **à partir du 2** — starting on the 2nd
- **le 1er septembre** — September 1st  
  Only the first uses **1er / premier**. Other days stay as the number: **le 2**, **le 30**.

Phone numbers in France are usually read in pairs:

- **06 12 34 56 78** — zéro six, douze, trente-quatre, cinquante-six, soixante-dix-huit

## Sound traps

- **six** and **dix** often sound like **see** and **dee** before a consonant: **six livres**, **dix jours**
- **cinq** often sounds like **sank** before a vowel and **san** before a consonant
- **vingt** is **van**; the **t** can appear in liaison: **vingt et un** — van-tay-uhn
- **un** is both “a/an” and “one”: **un livre**; **numéro un**

## Quick check

Build these from the layers, do not memorize them as isolated words:

- **34** = **trente-quatre**
- **71** = **soixante et onze**
- **80** = **quatre-vingts**
- **93** = **quatre-vingt-treize**
- **108** = **cent huit**
- **240** = **deux cent quarante**
